The Nostrion identity. Teal is `#40E0D0`.

- `Nostrion icon (on light).svg` and `(on dark).svg`: the three-bar logomark, for white and for dark backgrounds
- `Nostrion lockup (on light).svg` and `(on dark).svg`: icon plus the NOSTRION wordmark, outlined, so no font is needed
- `Nostrion icon 1024.png`: raster of the on-light icon, for app icons and favicons
